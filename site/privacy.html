Overview

Daily Hadith is an offline audio app for listening to reviewed Urdu hadith recordings with English reading text. The app is designed to work without accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Data we collect

Daily Hadith does not collect personal data from you. We do not create user accounts, collect contact information, sell data, or use third-party analytics SDKs.

Data stored on your device

The app may store listening progress, saved playback positions, reminder preferences, and acknowledgement of in-app notices locally on your device. This information stays on your device and is used only to make the app experience work.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. Nothing is sent to a server.

External links

The Settings screen includes a charity link to Islamic Relief. If you open an external website, that website is governed by its own privacy policy and practices.

Children’s privacy

Daily Hadith does not knowingly collect data from children or any other users.

Contact

For privacy questions, corrections, or support, email hasaamb@gmail.com.
